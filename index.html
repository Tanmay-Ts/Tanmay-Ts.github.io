RESEARCH / A WORK IN PROGRESS

LLM EVALUATION

Explanation ≠ execution.

Does being able to explain a formal rule mean a model can reliably carry it out?

DESIGN

900 preregistered instances

MODELS

Llama 3.1 & Gemini 2.0 Flash

The experiment

Three complexity levels and three token distributions, comparing formal-rule explanations with actual rule execution.

The part that matters

An annotation audit found 45.5% inter-rater agreement. I demoted the headline result and rebuilt the pipeline instead of treating an unreliable measurement as a conclusion.

Submitted to ACL Rolling Review, targeting BlackboxNLP 2026. This is ongoing research, not an accepted-publication claim.
